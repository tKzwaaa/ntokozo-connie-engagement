A Malawian tradition

Have you heard of Pelekani Pelekani?

It's time to experience a little something about the bride to be's Malawian culture!

Pelekani Pelekani is a joyful tradition of giving, celebrating, and blessing the bride and groom as they begin their new life together.

And yes, it will be happening at our engagement celebration. When the music starts, don't just watch the fun, join in! Come ready to dance, celebrate, and shower the newlyweds with your love and monetary gifts.

Not a cash-on-hand person?

Don't worry, we've got you covered! A gift registry is also available for those who would prefer to bless the newlyweds with a special gift.
